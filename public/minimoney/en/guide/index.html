How to set a monthly budget that starts on payday

Choose a monthly budget, align its start date with payday, and check how much you have left.

1. Choose a monthly budget

Look at what you spent last month to set an amount you can work with. If you include rent in that amount, record rent as spending against the same budget when you pay it.

2. Start your budget on payday

If you get paid on the 25th, start your budget on the 25th. A budget that starts on October 25 covers spending through November 24. In MiniMoney, you can set the amount and start day in your monthly budget settings.

3. Check how much is left

If you budget ₩1,500,000 and spend ₩450,000, you have ₩1,050,000 left. If you add ₩100,000 in extra income to the budget, you have ₩1,150,000 left. Try it with your own amounts
